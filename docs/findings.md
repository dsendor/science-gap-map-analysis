## What this shows

### AI maturity tracks verifiability, not importance

Share of each capability type's primary assignments that are already **working now**:

| Primary AI type | Working now | Total | Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| LLM reasoning and synthesis | 6 | 10 | 60% |
| Autonomous experimentation | 2 | 5 | 40% |
| ML surrogates and prediction | 6 | 20 | 30% |
| Sensing and signal processing | 3 | 15 | 20% |
| Design and optimization search | 4 | 21 | 19% |
| Coordination and institutional | 1 | 15 | 7% |
| Physical build and manipulation | 0 | 17 | 0% |

The gradient runs from **LLM reasoning and synthesis** at 60% down to **Physical build and manipulation** at 0%. Not one gap in the entire map has physical build and manipulation as a working-now primary: all 17 of them sit at 2-5 years or speculative. Coordination and institutional manages 1 of 15.

This is the ordering the verifier's-law framing predicts. Where feedback is fast and objective, AI has arrived. Where it is slow, contested, or absent, it has not.

### Where the cognitive tools have arrived, the field often cannot tell whether they helped

LLM reasoning and synthesis is the primary capability for only 10 of 103 gaps, and 6 of those are working now — the highest working-now share of any type. But 4 of those 6 sit outside tier 1, mostly at proxy-only measurability.

Metascience is the clearest instance. Its two gaps with a working-now LLM primary, literature synthesis and fraud detection, are both proxy-only: retractions measure detection capability, not fraud prevalence, and synthesis benchmarks do not measure whether knowledge became cumulative. Its one gap with a purely institutional primary is the map's only counterfactual-required case.

### The expected finding, tested

The prediction recorded before labeling was that LLM-shaped work would prove largely saturated and that most remaining gaps would be limited by physical build, fabrication, or institutions. **Confirmed, and by a sharper mechanism than expected.** Physical build, institutional coordination and autonomous experimentation together take 37 of 103 primaries (36%), against 10 for LLM reasoning. The stronger result is not the count but the maturity gradient above: the constraint is not that few gaps are cognitive, it is that the non-cognitive categories never reach working-now at all.

### A category that does not work

The audit found that **Proxy only cannot be applied reliably.** It ran 78% disagreement against 0% for Directly measurable and 9% for Verification contested, and all three auditors independently reported it was repeatedly the nearest alternative and almost never won. Every Proxy only assignment has therefore been downgraded to `guess`, including the ones the auditor did not sample.

This matters for reading everything above: the tiers that carry the argument are the two that held up. Tier 1 and tier 3 are reliable; the residual category between them is not, and a future version should either define it more sharply or drop it.

### Taxonomy gaps found by audit

Three cases where the seven-type taxonomy has no right answer, all surfaced independently by blind auditors:

- **Closed-loop control of a physical system** has no home. Fusion plasma control chooses no experiments, so it is not autonomous experimentation, and it builds nothing, so it is not physical build. This is a defensible eighth category.
- **Gaps where AI is the object rather than the instrument** (AI misuse, rogue AI, narrow reasoning) have no safety-and-robustness-research bucket.
- **Composite gaps** bundle sub-problems that would take different types *and different tiers*, so a single primary loses real information.
